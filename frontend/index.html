SOSForge -- live earthquake, tsunami and disaster tracker

SOSForge aggregates official public feeds into a single live stream of earthquakes, tsunamis, volcanic activity, tropical cyclones and disaster alerts, pushed to the browser over a websocket with a one-second heartbeat.

Where the data comes from

  • Earthquakes: EMSC (websocket push), USGS, GEOFON, JMA, BMKG, GeoNet, INGV, AFAD, CENC, NRCan, NOA, PHIVOLCS, SSN, IGP, IMO Iceland and NCS India
  • Earthquake early warning: JMA, issued while the seismic waves are still travelling
  • Aftershock forecasts: USGS
  • Tsunamis: NOAA NTWC and PTWC bulletins, JMA tsunami forecasts
  • Volcanoes: USGS HANS with the Smithsonian catalogue, GeoNet, IMO, JMA volcanic warnings, and volcanic-ash SIGMETs
  • Cyclones, floods, wildfires and other hazards: NOAA NHC, GDACS, NASA EONET, NWS, Meteoalarm, WMO
  • Space weather: NOAA SWPC

The raw feed is available without JavaScript: /api/events returns the recent events as JSON, and /api/sources reports the live health of every source.

SOSForge for Android: the same live feed as an app that watches your places in the background and notifies you.
